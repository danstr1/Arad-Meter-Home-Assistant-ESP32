Getting started

  1. Over Wi-Fi: open http://arad.local/ (or the IP shown on the board's Receiver screen page), or type that address above and click Connect (Wi-Fi).
  2. Over USB (first setup, or when Wi-Fi is not set): plug the board in, click USB and pick the board's port. Chrome or Edge only.
  3. Open Settings (appears after connecting) and enter your Wi-Fi and, if you use Home Assistant, your MQTT broker. Settings are saved on the board.
  4. Wait for meters to appear (each meter transmits about every 30 s). Give your own meter a name so it gets its own screen page.
Meter IDs are printed on the meter's radio module. Nothing is sent anywhere except to your own MQTT broker. This page stores history only in this browser.
